2How far one hand-written rule can go

人寫的一條規則能走多遠

Before the computer learns anything, we first guess with hand-written rules (rules that people thought of). The share of people we guess right is called accuracy, and every “score” below means accuracy. The simplest way is to guess “transported” for everyone. This is called a baseline: a starting point to compare with. The other two rules are the Week 11 rule, “guess transported for people in cryosleep,” and “guess transported for people who spent nothing” §〇 B. Guess first, then pick a rule to see its score.

還沒讓電腦學之前，先用人想的規則猜。猜對的人占全部的比例，叫準確率（accuracy），下面講的「分數」都是它。最簡單的做法是全部猜「被傳送」，這叫基準（baseline），是拿來比較的起點。另外兩條是 W11 那條「冬眠的人猜被傳送」，和「沒花錢的人猜被傳送」§〇 B。先猜，再選一條規則看分數。

Both rules give an answer for every person: people who match the condition are guessed “transported,” and everyone else is guessed “not transported.”

這兩條規則都會替每個人作答：符合條件的猜「被傳送」，其餘的猜「沒被傳送」。

Which is more accurate: “guess transported for people in cryosleep” or “guess transported for people who spent nothing at all”?「冬眠的人猜被傳送」跟「完全沒花錢的人猜被傳送」，哪一條比較準？

The spent-nothing rule: 6,408 people right, 0.7371. The cryosleep rule: 6,244 people right, 0.7183 §〇 B. Use the buttons below to switch, and see which groups each rule splits people into.

沒花錢那條：猜對 6,408 人、0.7371；冬眠那條猜對 6,244 人、0.7183 §〇 B。用下面的按鈕切換，看兩條規則把人分成哪幾群。

先猜上面那一題，再按一條規則，看它把人分成哪幾群、拿幾分。

Bar length = how many people are in that group. The dark part = the people in that group who were transported. All counts are real counts from the 8,693 people in train.csv §〇 B.

長條的長度＝那一群有幾個人；深色那一段＝那一群裡被傳送的人。人數都是 train.csv 8,693 人的真實計數 §〇 B。

Why have a baseline first? What score did the rule get on Kaggle? 為什麼要先有基準？規則交上 Kaggle 拿幾分？

The baseline is the score you get without looking at anything. In this competition the answers are about half and half, so the baseline is about 0.50. If a method's accuracy is not higher than 0.5036, it has not beaten “guess the same answer for everyone” §〇 B.

基準是什麼都不看也能拿到的分數。這場答案一半一半，所以基準大約 0.50；一個做法的準確率如果沒有比 0.5036 高，就是沒有贏過「全部猜同一個答案」§〇 B。

All the scores above come from checking answers on train.csv itself. On 11/16 the cryosleep rule was submitted to Kaggle. There it guessed the people in test.csv, and its public score was 0.72550 §〇 J. The rule scored about the same on two different groups of people (0.7183 and 0.72550) §〇 B §〇 J. The rule is only one sentence and does not remember any single passenger. That may be why it scores about the same on a new group of people ⚠️ our inference.

上面這些分數，都是在 train.csv 自己身上對答案。冬眠那條規則 11/16 交上 Kaggle，猜的是 test.csv 的人，公開分數 0.72550 §〇 J。這條規則在兩批不同的人身上分數很接近（0.7183 與 0.72550）§〇 B §〇 J。規則只有一句話、沒有記任何一位乘客，這可能是它換一批人還站得住的原因 ⚠️ 推論。

冬眠欄有 217 人是空的，其中 106 人被傳送 §〇 B。冬眠那條規則遇到空格，也猜沒被傳送。

3Training = letting the computer find rules

訓練＝讓電腦自己找規則

Training means letting the computer use data that has answers to work out its own way to guess. The way it works out is called a model. A decision tree is a series of yes-or-no questions. On every column, the computer tries every possible cut point and picks the question that best puts “transported” and “not transported” on two different sides. The next question looks only at the people who went down that side §〇 C. Guess first below: will its first question be “in cryosleep or not”?

訓練（training）就是讓電腦拿有答案的資料，自己整理出一套猜法，這套猜法叫模型（model）。決策樹（decision tree）是一連串是非題：電腦把每一欄可能的分界都試一遍，挑最能把「被傳送」和「沒被傳送」分到兩邊的那一題；下一題只看走到那一邊的人 §〇 C。下面先猜：它第一題會不會挑「有沒有冬眠」？

The computer can pick any column for its first question. Do you think it will pick “in cryosleep or not”?電腦可以挑任何一欄當第一題。你覺得它會挑「有沒有冬眠」嗎？

It picks “total spend ≤ 0.5,” which means whether the person spent any money §〇 C. This is the same rule as the third rule in Section 2, and on train.csv it also scores 0.7371 §〇 C. The computer does not know what “cryosleep” means. It just picks by “which question separates the two kinds of people the most.” By the way, this question is also more accurate than the cryosleep rule's 0.7183 §〇 B, but that is not why it was picked.

它挑「總消費 ≤ 0.5」，也就是有沒有花錢 §〇 C。這跟第 2 節第三條規則是同一條，在 train.csv 上一樣是 0.7371 §〇 C。電腦不知道「冬眠」是什麼意思，它只是照「哪一題把兩種人分得最開」去挑。順帶一提，這一題的準確率也比冬眠那條的 0.7183 高 §〇 B，但那不是它挑這一題的原因。

The two numbers in each leaf are how many people ended up there: “not transported｜transported.” Each leaf guesses the answer that more of its people have. The left line = “yes,” and the right line = “no” §〇 C.

The tree is scikit-learn's DecisionTreeClassifier, fed the 15 columns prepared in Section 6 §〇 C. All three trees in this section were trained on all 8,693 people and then checked against the same 8,693 people: one question 0.7371, two questions 0.7386, three questions 0.7584 §〇 C.

樹是 scikit-learn 的 DecisionTreeClassifier，餵第 6 節整理好的 15 欄 §〇 C。這一節的三棵樹都用全部 8,693 人訓練，再拿同樣這 8,693 人對答案：問一題 0.7371、兩題 0.7386、三題 0.7584 §〇 C。

The “practice questions” (the rows the model trained on) in the Section 4 table are measured another way: we split into five parts, check answers on the four training parts, and average over five rounds. So two questions shows 0.7389 and three questions shows 0.7592 §〇 C, a tiny bit different from here. Both are scores where the answers were seen.

第 4 節表格裡的「練習題」是另一種量法：切五份，拿訓練用的那四份自己對答案、五輪取平均，所以問兩題寫 0.7389、問三題寫 0.7592 §〇 C，跟這裡差一點點。兩個都是「看過答案」的分數。

During preparation, HomePlanet was changed into codes in alphabetical order: Earth 0, Europa 1, Mars 2 §〇 E. So among people who filled it in, “HomePlanet ≤ 0.5” means Earth, and “> 0.5” means Europa or Mars. 201 people left this column blank §〇 A. Which side the tree put them on, we did not check this time §〇 ⚠️7.

HomePlanet 在整理時換成代號，照字母順序 Earth 0、Europa 1、Mars 2 §〇 E；所以「HomePlanet ≤ 0.5」在有填的人裡就是 Earth，「> 0.5」是 Europa 或 Mars。這一欄沒填的 201 人 §〇 A，被樹歸到哪一邊，這次沒查 §〇 ⚠️7。

How people with blanks are split: during training, scikit-learn tries two ways at every possible cut point (all the people with blanks go left, or all go right) and keeps the one that separates better (scikit-learn 1.6 official explanation) §〇 ⚠️3. Which way the people with blanks actually went in these three trees, we did not check this time §〇 ⚠️7.

空格的人怎麼分邊：scikit-learn 在訓練時，每個可能的分界都試兩種走法（空格的人全部走左邊，或全部走右邊），留下分得比較好的那一種（scikit-learn 1.6 官方說明）§〇 ⚠️3。這三棵樹裡空格的人實際走了哪邊，這次沒查 §〇 ⚠️7。

How the computer judges “how well separated”: the measure is called Gini impurity. In plain words, it is “how mixed up the two kinds of answers are in this group of people.” It picks the question that makes the two sides, taken together, the least mixed (the side with more people counts more). It does not compare accuracy directly §〇 C.

電腦怎麼評「分得多開」：用的演算法叫吉尼不純度（Gini impurity），白話是「這一堆人裡兩種答案混得多亂」；它挑讓兩邊合起來最不混的那一題（人多的那一邊算得比較重），不是直接比準確率 §〇 C。

4Memorizing versus learning

背答案與學會的差別

A tree can keep asking more questions. The more questions, the higher the score on the practice questions (the rows it trained on). But on unseen questions (rows held out from training), the score mostly rises first and then falls, and it is highest at 6 questions §〇 C. This is called overfitting: memorizing the practice questions, then doing worse on a new set of questions. Drag the slider and watch the two lines move apart.
