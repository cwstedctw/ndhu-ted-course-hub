常見問題FAQ

一定要登入 AI 工具嗎？Must I sign in to an AI tool?

不用。頁內離線 Agent 與 app builder 就能完成共同核心。live AI 只是選配。No. The offline agent and app builder complete the shared core. Live AI is optional.

我需要看懂 HTML 或安裝編輯器嗎？Must I understand HTML or install an editor?

不用。首版只在白名單欄位改內容，再由頁面產生安全的單檔 app。No. In this prototype, you edit only approved fields and the page generates a safe single-file app.

為什麼不讓 AI 直接重寫整份程式？Why not let AI rewrite the whole program?

這週先練「看差異、測試、批准與復原」。任意程式碼會讓安全與除錯變成主要負擔，反而看不到這四件事。This week focuses on reading changes, testing, approval, and recovery. Arbitrary code would turn security and debugging into the main task.

進度會被老師拿去評分嗎？Is the progress bar graded?

不會。它只在這台電腦保存匿名勾選狀態。真正證據是你下載或列印的內容。No. It stores only anonymous checkmarks on this computer. Your downloaded or printed evidence is what counts.

英文寫得不漂亮會扣分嗎？Will imperfect English lower my score?

不會。本週評修改、測試、人的決定與風險判斷，不評英文華麗程度。AA 可用頁面句型與繁中支援。No. This week assesses editing, testing, human decisions, and risk judgment—not polished English. AA students may use the sentence frames and Chinese support.
