How to use it怎麼用

  1. Double-click the page to open it. It works without the internet.雙擊打開網頁，不用網路也能用。
  2. Choose from 40 activities with three filters: location, time and energy.用地點、時間、體力三個條件，從 40 個活動裡挑。
  3. Press “Pick for me”. It picks one matching activity at random. If none match, it says “No matching activities.”按「幫我選」，隨機抽一個符合的活動；都不符合就顯示「沒有符合條件的活動」。
  4. Your last 5 picks are listed, newest first. “Clear history” empties the list; “Reset filters” keeps it.最近 5 次會列出來，最新在上；「清除紀錄」清空，「重設條件」不會清。
  5. Sign in to save your picks to the cloud.登入後可以把選擇存到雲端。

Things it cannot do它做不到的事

None.沒有。
