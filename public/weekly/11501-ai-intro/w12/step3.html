1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does each piece (token) use itself and the pieces that came before it?

每一小塊文字（token）怎麼利用自己和前面的塊？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

Now the pieces of Hualien is famous for look at each other. At ▁for, the model may look at itself and every earlier piece (H, ual, ien, ▁is, ▁famous), and nothing after it. Which earlier word do you think matters most for guessing what comes next? (We don't print percentages for this sentence: any number we wrote would be made up.)

同一句的每一塊開始互相看。讀到 for 時，只能看自己和前面的塊（H、ual、ien、is、famous），後面的看不到。你覺得前面哪一塊最能幫它猜下一塊？（這句我們不寫百分比，寫了也是編的。）

2Guess first

先猜

In “…because it was too big”, when the model is at “it”, can it already see “big”?

在「…because it was too big」裡，模型讀到 it 的時候，看得到後面的 big 嗎？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

No. The causal mask hides every later word, so at “it” the word “big” has weight 0. “big” does the linking when it arrives. 看不到；因果遮罩把後面的字擋住，要等 big 出現再回頭連。

6Run the real model (Colab)

用真的模型跑（Colab）

This step uses Gemma 3 270M, a much smaller model from the same family: the idea is the same, the numbers are different. 這一段用的是同家族、小很多的 Gemma 3 270M：道理一樣、數字不一樣。 Run Step 3 Attention in the notebook. It feeds in “The trophy didn't fit in the suitcase because it was too big.” and draws bars for how much “it” looks at each earlier piece (last layer, average of its heads). When we ran it on 11/23, most of the weight went to the start mark <bos> (0.72) and trophy got only 0.027, not the textbook story; yours may differ slightly. Describe honestly what you see, then try another layer. 跑筆記本的 Step 3，會看到 it 往前看每一塊的長條。我們 11/23 那次最多落在句首記號 <bos>（0.72），trophy 只有 0.027，跟課本故事不一樣；你的可能略有不同。請照實描述，再換一層試試。
