第 3 站 · 注意力與層

1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does each piece (token) use itself and the pieces that came before it?

每一小塊文字（token）怎麼利用自己和前面的塊？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

Now the pieces of Hualien is famous for look at each other. At ▁for, the model may look at itself and every earlier piece (H, ual, ien, ▁is, ▁famous), and nothing after it. Which earlier word do you think matters most for guessing what comes next? This page did not measure this sentence in Gemma 4: the proportions in the toy and the video are illustrative. For real numbers, see the small-model run in Colab (section 6).

同一句的每一塊開始互相看。讀到 for 時，只能看自己和前面的塊（H、ual、ien、is、famous），後面的看不到。你覺得前面哪一塊最能幫它猜下一塊？（這一頁沒有拿這句去量 Gemma 4，玩具與短片裡的比例是示意；真的數字請看 Colab 小模型那次。）

2Guess first

先猜

In “…because it was too big”, when the model is at “it”, can it already see “big”?

在「…because it was too big」裡，模型讀到 it 的時候，看得到後面的 big 嗎？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

No. The causal mask hides every later word, so at “it” the word “big” has weight 0. “big” does the linking when it arrives. 看不到；因果遮罩把後面的字擋住，要等 big 出現再回頭連。

Transcript 逐幕文字（English narration + 中文字幕）

  1. How does Gemma 4 know which earlier words matter most? Alone, the word for gives almost no clue what comes next.

    模型怎麼知道前面哪個字重要？光看 for 根本猜不出後面。

  2. Picture a classroom vote where the latest speaker looks back and gives more votes to helpful classmates. That scoring is called attention.

    想成課堂投票：最新的字回頭看前面，這套打分叫注意力。

  3. In this made-up example, at the word for, it casts strong votes for Hualien and famous, and a tiny vote for is, blending their meaning together.

    在這個示意例子裡，for 會給花蓮和 famous 高票，再混成新意思。

  4. There is one strict rule: words can only look at themselves and backward, never ahead. A one-way curtain hides future tokens, called a causal mask.

    而且只能看自己和前面、不能偷看後面，這道遮簾叫因果遮罩。

  5. One vote is not enough. Gemma 4 repeats this round inside a stack of layers—35 in E2B, up to 60 in 31B.

    E2B 有 35 層，31B 有 60 層。

  6. To save memory, most layers only check nearby words. Every fifth or sixth layer, and always the last, lets each piece look at itself and all earlier pieces.

    每隔五、六層以及最後一層，每一塊可以看自己和前面所有的塊。

  7. When for exits the final layer, its vector holds the full Hualien context. Now Gemma 4 is ready to guess what comes next.

    走出最後一層，句尾的向量已經整合了前面的資訊，接著拿來猜下一塊。
