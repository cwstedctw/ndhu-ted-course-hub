1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How do Steps 1–4 become a chat that answers you?

第 1–4 站怎麼變成會回你話的聊天？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

The 5th stage in the official pipeline diagram is this loop; the official Stage 5 page looks at it through chat. Answering questions well comes from post-training plus the chat format, not from the markers alone.

Press Step in the toy below: Hualien is famous for (6 real pieces) grows one piece per run, and each new piece is fed back into the input. In a real chat, your message is first wrapped in the chat template with turn markers such as <|turn> (ID 105) and <turn|> (ID 106); the guide says the prompt handed to the model ends at <|turn>model plus a line break. Then the loop runs: Step 4 draws one piece, the piece is added to the input, and the model continues from Step 2 (looking up the new piece's vector); it does not cut the whole sentence again. With standard token-by-token generation, writing 20 tokens takes about 20 runs. This example stops at <turn|> (106); generation can also stop at <eos> (1).

官方管線圖的第 5 階段就是這個迴圈，官方第 5 頁用聊天來看它。能回答問題靠的是後訓練（post-training）加上聊天格式，不是只靠記號。

按下面玩具的 Step：同一句（真的切法是 6 塊）每跑一次長一塊，新的一塊接回輸入。真的聊天裡，你的訊息會先被包進聊天格式，用 <|turn>（105）和 <turn|>（106）這類記號分段；導覽說交給模型的提示停在 <|turn>model 加一個換行。接著迴圈開始：第 4 站抽出一塊、接回輸入，從第 2 站（查向量）繼續，不會把整句重新斷詞。標準的逐 token 生成寫 20 個 token 約跑 20 次。這個例子停在 <turn|>（106），一般也可能停在 <eos>（1）。

Real output from the Gemma 4 chat template, run in Colab on 2026-11-23

Asking “What is Hualien famous for?” turns into this one long text before Step 1 starts (it ends with a line break after <|turn>model): 問一句話，送進模型之前先變成這一長串（最後在 <|turn>model 後面有一個換行）：

<bos><|turn>user
What is Hualien famous for?<turn|>
<|turn>model

The model's answer is written after <|turn>model, one piece at a time, until it writes the end mark. 模型的回答從 <|turn>model 後面開始，一塊一塊寫，直到寫出結束記號。

⑤ The loop: one token at a time (Stages 4–5)

Scripted — choices and chances are made up

Each press of Step is one full run of the model. It guesses, picks one piece, and that piece is fed back onto the end of the input for the next run. This is the autoregressive loop. (A real chat would also wrap the input in the chat template; we skip that to keep it short.)

每按一次 Step＝模型完整跑一次：猜、挑一塊、把那一塊接回輸入尾巴，下一次再跑。這就是自迴歸迴圈。
