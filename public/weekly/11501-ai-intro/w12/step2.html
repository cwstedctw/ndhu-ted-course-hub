1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does an ID number turn into something with meaning?

一個編號怎麼變成帶有意思的東西？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

Each piece of Hualien is famous for now has an ID from Step 1. Here every ID looks up its own long list of numbers, called an embedding: 1,536 numbers per piece in E2B, 2,560 in E4B, 5,376 in 31B (from the guide). The same piece always starts with the same list, wherever it appears.

同一句的每一塊都有第 1 站的編號；這一站用編號去查一串很長的數字（嵌入向量）：E2B 每塊 1,536 個數字、E4B 2,560 個、31B 5,376 個。同一塊不管出現在哪，起始那串數字都一樣。

2Guess first

Which is closer to “cat”: “kitten” or “car”?

哪個比較接近 cat：kitten 還是 car？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

Kitten, in our toy below (score close to 1). The guide reports cat–kitten at 0.43 in Gemma 4 E2B's starting embedding table (Layer 0), lower than the toy value. In the toy, the score means: +1 = the two arrows point the same way, 0 = they are at right angles, −1 = opposite. 玩具裡 kitten 比較近（接近 1）；導覽說 E2B 起始嵌入表（Layer 0）裡這一對只有 0.43，比示意值低。分數的意思：+1 同方向、0 互相垂直、−1 相反。

② Meaning map: how close are two words? (Stage 2)

Made-up illustrative vectors

Real embeddings have thousands of directions (the guide gives 1,536 numbers per token in E2B, up to 5,376 in 31B), and nobody labels them. This 2-D map and these 3-number vectors are made up so you can see the idea: each word gets 3 invented numbers — how much it is an animal, a vehicle, a food. Similarity is the cosine similarity of those 3 numbers.

真正的嵌入向量有上千個方向（E2B 每個 token 有 1,536 個數字，31B 有 5,376 個），而且沒有人幫它們貼標籤。這張平面圖和每個字的 3 個數字都是編出來的，只是讓你看懂概念。

Compare with reality: the guide reports cat–kitten at only 0.43 in Gemma 4 E2B's starting embedding table (Layer 0) — lower than our toy's value. 跟真的比：導覽說 E2B 起始嵌入表（Layer 0）裡 cat 和 kitten 只有 0.43，這一對在真模型裡的分數比示意值低。

6Run the real model (Colab)

用真的模型跑（Colab）

This step uses Gemma 3 270M, a much smaller model from the same family: the idea is the same, the numbers are different. 這一段用的是同家族、小很多的 Gemma 3 270M：道理一樣、數字不一樣。 Run Step 2 Embeddings in the notebook. You will see the list of numbers for “cat” and a similarity table for cat, kitten, dog, car, truck, banana, tiger. When we ran it on 11/23, cat–dog was 0.80 but cat–kitten only 0.53, which is not what most people expect; yours may differ slightly. Why might a model put cat closer to dog than to kitten? Describe what you see. 跑筆記本的 Step 2，會看到 cat 的那串數字和一張相似度表。我們 11/23 那次 cat–dog 是 0.80、cat–kitten 只有 0.53，跟直覺不一樣；你的可能略有不同。想想為什麼，描述你看到的。
