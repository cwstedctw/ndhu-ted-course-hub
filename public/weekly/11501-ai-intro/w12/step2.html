1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does an ID number turn into something with meaning?

一個編號怎麼變成帶有意思的東西？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

Each piece of Hualien is famous for now has an ID from Step 1. Here every ID looks up its own long list of numbers, called an embedding: 1,536 numbers per piece in E2B, 2,560 in E4B, 5,376 in 31B (from the guide). The same piece always starts with the same list, wherever it appears.

同一句的每一塊都有第 1 站的編號；這一站用編號去查一串很長的數字（嵌入向量）：E2B 每塊 1,536 個數字、E4B 2,560 個、31B 5,376 個。同一塊不管出現在哪，起始那串數字都一樣。

2Guess first

先猜

Which is closer to “cat”: “kitten” or “car”?

哪個比較接近 cat：kitten 還是 car？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

In our toy below, both dog (1.00) and kitten (0.99) score very close to cat, and car is far. The guide reports cat–kitten at 0.43 in Gemma 4 E2B's starting embedding table (Layer 0), lower than the toy value. In the toy, the score means: +1 = the two arrows point the same way, 0 = they are at right angles, −1 = opposite. 玩具裡 dog（1.00）和 kitten（0.99）都跟 cat 很近，car 很遠；導覽說 E2B 起始嵌入表（Layer 0）裡這一對只有 0.43，比示意值低。分數的意思：+1 同方向、0 互相垂直、−1 相反。

Transcript 逐幕文字（English narration + 中文字幕）

  1. Why can't Gemma 4 understand a Token ID yet? ID 5866 for cat is just an arbitrary label, like a dictionary page number.

    為什麼光有編號還不懂意思？編號只是頁碼，沒有語意。

  2. Think of a map: every spot gets coordinates so nearby places sit together. Giving each ID meaning coordinates is called an embedding.

    想成一張地圖：每個字換成座標，這一步叫做嵌入。

  3. Each token gets a long list of numbers called a vector. Gemma 4 E2B uses 1,536 numbers, and 31B uses 5,376.

    這串數叫向量，維度從 1,536 到 5,376。

  4. On our sketch map, after reading massive text, similar meanings cluster on this map. Taroko lands near ocean and mountains, far away from banana and laptop.

    在我們畫的示意地圖上，模型看過大量文章後，意思接近的字會自動靠在一起。

  5. We measure closeness by the angle between vectors, called cosine similarity. In Gemma 4 E2B's starting table, king and queen score 0.58, cat and kitten 0.43.

    取夾角的餘弦叫餘弦相似度，國王配皇后是 0.58。

  6. One limit remains: every time a word appears, this map gives one fixed spot. It cannot tell river bank from money bank yet.

    但這只是固定起點：同一個字還沒看上下文，座標都一樣。

  7. Now every piece of our sentence holds map coordinates. Next, Step Three lets them talk to each other for context.

    現在句子裡每一塊都有了座標，下一站要讓它們互相看上下文。

② Meaning map: how close are two words? (Stage 2)

Made-up illustrative vectors

Real embeddings have thousands of directions (the guide gives 1,536 numbers per token in E2B, up to 5,376 in 31B), and nobody labels them. This 2-D map and these 3-number vectors are made up so you can see the idea: each word gets 3 invented numbers — how much it is an animal, a vehicle, a food. Similarity is the cosine similarity of those 3 numbers.

真正的嵌入向量有上千個方向（E2B 每個 token 有 1,536 個數字，31B 有 5,376 個），而且沒有人幫它們貼標籤。這張平面圖和每個字的 3 個數字都是編出來的，只是讓你看懂概念。

Compare with reality: the guide reports cat–kitten at only 0.43 in Gemma 4 E2B's starting embedding table (Layer 0) — lower than our toy's value. 跟真的比：導覽說 E2B 起始嵌入表（Layer 0）裡 cat 和 kitten 只有 0.43，這一對在真模型裡的分數比示意值低。
