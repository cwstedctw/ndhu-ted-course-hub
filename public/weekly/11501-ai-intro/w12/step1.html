“Hualien is famous for”: 6 pieces when we ran it (H｜ual｜ien｜▁is｜▁famous｜▁for). “2026”: four, plus one more: when we ran the real Gemma 4 tokenizer in Colab, in “I love 🍉 in 2026”, the number became one piece per digit (2｜0｜2｜6), with an extra space piece ▁ in front of it. The guide says the same rule (its example: “140” becomes 1, 4, 0). Chinese is not one piece per character: in 花蓮的海很藍, 的海 stuck together. Transcript 逐幕文字（English narration + 中文字幕）

  1. How does a computer read raw words like Hualien is famous for? Computers only do math on numbers, never directly on letters.

    電腦怎麼讀文字？電腦只會算數字，沒辦法直接算英文字母。

  2. Think of cooking: a chef chops whole ingredients into bite-sized pieces first. Cutting text into small pieces is called tokenization.

    就像煮菜前要先切菜，把文字切成小塊的步驟叫斷詞。

  3. Each piece is a token, and its fixed list number is a Token ID. All five Gemma 4 models share 262,144 numbers.

    每小塊叫 token，配上 0 到 262,143 號。

  4. Common words often stay in one piece, like cat at 5866 and the at 506, each with its leading space. Rare or long words often get chopped into smaller pieces.

    常見字常常整塊留著，像前面帶空格的 cat 是 5866；罕見字常被切細。

  5. Even an ultra-rare character never gets thrown away. If it is missing, Gemma 4 slices it into raw bytes instead of unknown.

    遇到詞表沒有的冷僻字也不丟掉，改切更細的位元組。

  6. Remember one key fact: chopping text uses zero AI thinking yet. It is just a fixed lookup table turning pieces into integer IDs.

    這一步還沒動到神經網路，只是照表查出整數編號。

  7. Next, those bare IDs need meaning. First, try your own sentence in the live demo and see how Gemma 4 chops it.

    光有編號還沒意義。先到官方頁打一句你自己的話，看它怎麼切。
