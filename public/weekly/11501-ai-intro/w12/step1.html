Will “2026” be one piece or four? Will a Chinese sentence be one piece per character?

「2026」會是一塊還是四塊？中文句子會一個字一塊嗎？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

Four, plus one more: when we ran the real Gemma 4 tokenizer in Colab, “2026” became one piece per digit (2｜0｜2｜6), with an extra space piece ▁ in front. The guide says the same rule (its example: “140” becomes 1, 4, 0). Chinese is not one piece per character: in 花蓮的海很藍, 的海 stuck together. Open the real split below, then check it yourself in the official box. 真的斷詞器把 2026 切成一位數一塊，前面還多一個空格塊；中文不是一字一塊，「的海」黏在一起。看下面真的切法，再去官方框自己打一次。

4Play

① Try these in the official tokenizer (Stage 1)

Stage 1 of the official guide runs Gemma 4's real tokenizer, so there is no fake toy here. Type each input below into the official Stage 1 box. Next to each one is what we saw when we ran the same tokenizer in Colab on 2026-11-23; check that the official page shows the same.

官方第 1 站跑的就是 Gemma 4 真正的斷詞器，所以這裡不做假的。把下面每一句打進官方第 1 站的框；每句後面是我們 11/23 在 Colab 跑同一個斷詞器看到的結果，對照官方頁是不是一樣。

  1. Hualien is famous for — we saw 6 pieces: H｜ual｜ien｜▁is｜▁famous｜▁for. A rare place name is cut into chunks; common words often stay whole. 罕見的地名被切成幾塊，常用字各自一塊。
  2. The cat sat on the mat. — we saw 7 pieces, IDs 818, 5866, 2838, 580, 506, 1756, 236761 (the same list the guide gives). 7 塊，編號跟導覽寫的一樣。
  3. 花蓮的海很藍 — 6 characters but 5 pieces: 的海 sticks together as one piece. 6 個字但只有 5 塊：「的海」黏成一塊。
  4. 加鬮 — 加 is one piece; the rare 鬮 becomes three byte pieces <0xE9> <0xAC> <0xAE>. 鬮變成三個位元組塊。
  5. I love 🍉 in 2026 — the emoji is one piece with a separate space piece ▁ before it; 2026 is one piece per digit, also with a space piece in front. 西瓜一塊、前面有獨立的空格塊；2026 一位數一塊，前面也多一個空格塊。

Write the most surprising one in box 1 of your five-box card. Words to check first: token, subword tokenization, Token ID.
