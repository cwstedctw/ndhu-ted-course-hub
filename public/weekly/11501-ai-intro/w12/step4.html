1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does the model pick the next piece?

模型怎麼挑下一塊？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

After the layers, the model gives a raw score (a logit) to every entry in its 262,144-piece vocabulary for “what comes after Hualien is famous for”. The order is: temperature first adjusts the scores; softmax turns them into chances; Top-K keeps the K most likely and rescales them to 100%; Top-P adds up those chances from the top and keeps them until the total first reaches or passes P; the survivors are rescaled to 100% again; then one piece is drawn. This step shows three kinds of examples: the numbers in the video and the toy are made up; the Colab numbers come from a real small model. Gemma 4's real defaults (from the guide): temperature 1.0, top_k 64, top_p 0.95.

經過各層之後，模型替詞表 262,144 塊裡的每一塊打一個原始分數（logit），猜 “Hualien is famous for” 後面接什麼。順序是：溫度先調整分數，softmax 再換成機率，接著 Top-K 只留 K 個並重新算成 100%，Top-P 在這份機率上由高到低累加、留到第一次達到或超過 P，再重新算成 100%，最後抽一塊。這一站有三種例子：短片和玩具的數字是編的，Colab 的是小模型實跑。Gemma 4 的真實預設：溫度 1.0、top_k 64、top_p 0.95。

2Guess first

先猜

If temperature is very low, will you get the same word every time?

溫度調到很低，每次都會拿到同一個字嗎？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

At temperature 0 (greedy), yes: always the top choice. Very low but above 0 gives the top choice almost every time, when the top choice is clearly ahead. Try it in toy ④. 溫度 0（greedy）每次都一樣；很低但不是 0 時，首選明顯領先的話幾乎每次一樣。

Transcript 逐幕文字（English narration + 中文字幕）

  1. How does Gemma 4 pick what follows Hualien is famous for? First, it scores all 262,144 tokens and turns scores into percentages.

    怎麼挑下一個字？先把 262,144 個詞打分轉成機率。

  2. Imagine a lucky-draw jar where higher percentages get more tickets. Always picking number one makes answers robotic, so the model draws a ticket.

    想成抽籤筒：機率越高籤越多，不永遠選第一，而是抽籤。

  3. Temperature changes the scores first, before they become percentages. Its default is 1.0: lower sharpens the top choice, higher spreads chances out.

    溫度先調整分數，再換成機率；預設 1.0，越低越保守，越高越有變化。

  4. Next, we toss wild guesses out of the jar. Keeping only a fixed count of top words is called Top-K—64 by default.

    接著只留前幾名候選叫 Top-K，預設留前 64 個。

  5. Then we keep just enough top words to reach a target share of tickets. That flexible cutoff is called Top-P, defaulting to 0.95.

    Top-P 從機率最高的開始加，達到門檻就停

  6. The surviving tickets reset to 100%, and Gemma 4 draws one winner—say, Taroko. That winner joins the prompt to repeat the loop.

    留下的籤重算成 100% 後抽出一張，接回原句繼續跑。

  7. Same scores, different dials, different chances for each answer. Next, Step Five shows how this word-by-word draw turns into a real chat.

    同一批分數，調整設定會改變每個答案被抽中的機會；下站看聊天。
