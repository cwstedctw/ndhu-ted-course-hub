1The question this step answers

這一站回答的問題

How does the model pick the next piece?

模型怎麼挑下一塊？

Our sentence at this step: “Hualien is famous for” · 同一句走到這一站

After the layers, the model gives a raw score (a logit) to every entry in its 262,144-piece vocabulary for “what comes after Hualien is famous for”. The order is: temperature first adjusts the scores; softmax turns them into chances; Top-K keeps the K most likely; Top-P keeps candidates from the top until the running total first reaches or passes P; then one piece is drawn. In our made-up Step 5 toy, Taroko gets 41%, its 33%, the 14%. Those numbers are invented: when we ran a real small Gemma model in Colab on 11/23, it chose “ its” almost every time (98.56%). Gemma 4's real defaults (from the guide): temperature 1.0, top_k 64, top_p 0.95.

經過各層之後，模型替詞表 262,144 塊裡的每一塊打一個原始分數（logit），猜 “Hualien is famous for” 後面接什麼。順序是：溫度先調整分數，softmax 再換成機率，接著 Top-K 留前 K 名、Top-P 從最高的往下加到累積機率第一次達到或超過 P，最後抽一塊。在第 5 站的示意資料中，Taroko 41%、its 33%、the 14%，這是編的；我們 11/23 在 Colab 跑真的小模型，幾乎都接 “ its”（98.56%）。Gemma 4 的真實預設：溫度 1.0、top_k 64、top_p 0.95。

2Guess first

If temperature is very low, will you get the same word every time?

溫度調到很低，每次都會拿到同一個字嗎？

Write your guess on paper, then open the answer. 先猜再點開

At temperature 0 (greedy), yes: always the top choice. Very low but above 0 gives the top choice almost every time, when the top choice is clearly ahead. Try it in toy ④. 溫度 0（greedy）每次都一樣；很低但不是 0 時，首選明顯領先的話幾乎每次一樣。

用真的模型跑（Colab）
