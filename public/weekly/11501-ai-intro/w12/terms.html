How to use this page with the official guide

怎麼搭配官方導覽使用

  1. Open both. Official guide in one window, this page in another (or phone + laptop). 兩個視窗並排。
  2. On the official page, pick a stage in the row of buttons at the top (“01 Tokenization” … “05 Chat & tools”) to open its live demo; press “>_ Read the details” when you want the long explanation. 在官方頁的站別列（rail）選站、點開下方說明面板。
  3. When you meet a word you don't know, type it into the search box of the term station. Start with ⭐ cards; 💤 cards are safe to skip. 看不懂的字就來這裡搜；先看 ⭐，💤 可以跳過。
  4. Each card's “Where you'll see it” names the stage and the heading you see after pressing “>_ Read the details” on the official page, so you can find the spot. 每張卡都寫了在官方頁哪一站、哪個標題底下。
  5. Write your card as you go (one sentence per stage). 邊走邊寫學習卡。

① Try these in the official tokenizer (Stage 1)

Stage 1 of the official guide already runs Gemma 4's real tokenizer, so there is no toy here — use the real thing. Type each input below into the official Stage 1 box and look closely.

官方第 1 站跑的就是 Gemma 4 真正的斷詞器，所以這裡不做假的，直接去用真的。把下面每一句打進官方第 1 站的輸入框，仔細看。

  1. The cat sat on the mat. — notice whether each common word becomes one piece, and whether the space shows up as a ▁ stuck to the front of the next word. 注意常用字是不是各自一塊、空格是不是變成黏在字前面的 ▁。
  2. unbelievable (or antidisestablishmentarianism) — notice whether it stays whole or splits into several reusable chunks, and where the cuts fall. 注意它有沒有被拆開、切在哪裡。
  3. 我在花蓮讀大學 — notice whether each Chinese character is its own piece or some characters join together; count pieces vs. characters. Then add the rare character 鬮 and notice whether it turns into byte pieces like <0xE9>. 注意中文是一字一塊還是會合併；再加一個罕用字「鬮」看看會不會變成位元組片段。
  4. 🚀🙂 — notice whether each emoji is one piece or several. 注意表情符號是一塊還是好幾塊。
  5. 2026 — notice whether the number is one piece or one piece per digit, and compare with 2,026. 注意數字是整塊還是一個位數一塊。

Write the most surprising one in box 1 of your card. Words to check first: token, subword tokenization, Token ID.

② Meaning map: how close are two words? (Stage 2)

Made-up illustrative vectors

Real embeddings have thousands of directions (the guide gives 1,536 numbers per token in E2B, up to 5,376 in 31B), and nobody labels them. This 2-D map and these 3-number vectors are made up so you can see the idea: each word gets 3 invented numbers — how much it is an animal, a vehicle, a food. Similarity is the cosine similarity of those 3 numbers.

真正的嵌入向量有上千個方向（E2B 每個 token 有 1,536 個數字，31B 有 5,376 個），而且沒有人幫它們貼標籤。這張平面圖和每個字的 3 個數字都是編出來的，只是讓你看懂概念。

Compare with reality: the guide reports cat–kitten at only 0.43 in Gemma 4 E2B's starting embedding table (Layer 0) — lower than our toy's value. 跟真的比：導覽說 E2B 起始嵌入表（Layer 0）裡 cat 和 kitten 只有 0.43，這一對在真模型裡的分數比示意值低。

④ Temperature, Top-K, Top-P (Stage 4)

Made-up scores (logits) for 8 candidates

Prompt: “After class, I'm going to eat …” Each candidate has a fixed made-up logit. Move the sliders and watch the chances change. Pale blue edge = chance after softmax only; solid bar = final chance after the cuts. Crossed-out = cut.

提示：「下課後我要去吃……」。每個候選有一個固定的假分數。拉滑桿看機率怎麼變；淺藍邊＝只經過 softmax 的機率，實心長條是篩選後的抽樣機率，劃掉的候選不參與抽樣。

Order used here: score ÷ temperature → softmax → Top-K keeps K and rescales to 100% → Top-P adds from the top until the running total first reaches or passes P → rescale to 100% → draw. 順序：分數除以溫度 → softmax → Top-K 留 K 個並重新算成 100% → Top-P 由高到低累加到第一次達到或超過 P → 再重新算成 100% → 抽。

Last 10 samples (newest first):

Gemma 4's real defaults (from the guide): temperature 1.0, top_k 64, top_p 0.95. Our toy only has 8 candidates, so K = 8 here already keeps everything, just like 64 would. 我們只有 8 個候選，K = 8 就等於全留。

⑤ The loop: one token at a time (Stages 4–5)

Scripted — choices and chances are made up

Each press of Step is one full run of the model. It guesses, picks one piece, and that piece is fed back onto the end of the input for the next run. This is the autoregressive loop. (A real chat would also wrap the input in the chat template; we skip that to keep it short.)

每按一次 Step＝模型完整跑一次：猜、挑一塊、把那一塊接回輸入尾巴，下一次再跑。這就是自迴歸迴圈。
