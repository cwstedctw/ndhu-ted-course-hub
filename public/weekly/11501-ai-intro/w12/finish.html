今天走過的五站

1Today's five steps, one sentence each

今天走過的五站，各一句話

  1. Step 1 · Tokenization
    Text is cut into pieces (tokens), and each piece gets an ID number from the vocabulary.
    文字被切成一小塊一小塊（token），每塊拿到詞表裡的一個編號。
  2. Step 2 · Embeddings
    Each ID looks up a list of numbers (a vector); pieces with related meanings point in similar directions.
    每個編號查到一串數字（向量）；意思相近的塊，方向也相近。
  3. Step 3 · Attention & layers
    Each piece looks at itself and the pieces before it, and decides how much each one matters.
    每一塊看自己和前面的塊，決定每一塊有多重要。
  4. Step 4 · Sampling
    The model scores every possible next piece; temperature, softmax, Top-K and Top-P shape the chances, then one piece is drawn.
    模型替每個可能的下一塊打分數；溫度、softmax、Top-K、Top-P 調整機率後抽出一塊。
  5. Step 5 · Chat, thinking & tools
    The new piece is added to the input and the loop runs again; a chat is the same loop, wrapped in the chat template.
    新的一塊接回輸入、迴圈再跑；聊天也是同一個迴圈，只是包進了對話範本。
