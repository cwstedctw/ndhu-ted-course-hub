0The question for today

今天的問題

An LLM only does one thing: guess the next piece of text. How does that become an answer?

大型語言模型只做一件事：猜下一小塊文字。這怎麼變成一個回答？

One sentence through all five steps · 一句話走完五站

The website and the videos follow one sentence: Hualien is famous for (the notebook's Steps 2 and 3 use cat and trophy examples instead). Step 1 cuts it into pieces, Step 2 turns each piece into a vector, Step 3 lets the pieces look at each other, Step 4 draws the next piece. That piece is added to the input and the model continues from Step 2 (looking up its vector); the sentence is not cut again. Step 5 shows this loop through chat.

網站和短片的主線是這一句：“Hualien is famous for”（筆記本第 2、3 段另用 cat 與 trophy 的例子）。第 1 站切成小塊、第 2 站每塊變成向量、第 3 站讓各塊互相看、第 4 站抽出下一塊；這一塊接回輸入，從第 2 站（查向量）繼續，不會整句重新斷詞。第 5 站用聊天來看這個迴圈。

▶Short video

看短片

Transcript 逐幕文字（English narration + 中文字幕）

  1. How does an AI write? Gemma 4 really does one job: predict the next small piece of text, called a token.

    AI 怎麼寫字？它只做一件事：猜下一個 token。

  2. Picture a relay race with five stations. Station four picks the next token, and station five sends it back into the race.

    想成五站接力：第四站挑出下一塊，第五站把它接回去再跑。

  3. Station one chops Hualien is famous for into numbered pieces from 262,144 choices. Station two turns each number into meaning coordinates.

    第一站切文字並給編號，第二站把編號換成語意座標。

  4. At station three, each piece weighs itself and the earlier pieces. At station four, Gemma 4 scores every choice and draws one winner.

    第三站每一塊回頭衡量自己和前面的線索，第四站幫候選打分再抽籤。

  5. At station five, that new token joins the input and runs the relay again, looping back to station two until the reply ends.

    第五站把新字接回原句，從第二站重跑，直到回答結束。

  6. All five Gemma 4 models run this exact relay, from E2B to 31B. Their reading limit, or context window, reaches 128K or 256K.

    五種型號都跑這套，上下文上限 128K 或 256K。

  7. One simple loop powers everything the model writes. Follow Hualien is famous for through all five stations, starting at Station One.

    就靠這個循環撐起整個模型，現在從第一站跟著這句出發。
