W2 Image judgment studio

Artificial Intelligence AA · 14 September 2026 · Prompts, images and human judgment

Use a campus poster to practice making a request, inspecting an image and deciding whether to adopt, modify or reject it. You do not need a perfect image. You need evidence for your decision and a sensible next step.

用一張校園海報，練習提問、看圖與決定。作品可以不完美，判斷要有依據。

The brief / 活動需求

Design a portrait poster for a fictional campus book swap, inviting first-year students, including international students. This is not an actual announcement, venue reservation or officially endorsed event.

這是虛構換書活動，不是實際公告，沒有場地預約或校方背書。

ItemFixed information
Title / 標題BOOK SWAP
Date / 日期22 SEPT 2026
Time / 時間16:30
Place / 地點LIBRARY LOBBY / 圖書館大廳
Participation / 參與方式BRING ONE BOOK, TAKE ONE BOOK
Status / 性質FICTIONAL EVENT / CLASS EXERCISE

You may change the color, illustration, tagline or layout. Keep the event facts. Do not add a school logo, real person, sponsor, official endorsement or registration link.

可以改視覺呈現；不能自行改活動事實或加入背書。

Five checkpoints / 五個停靠點

  • Choose mockup A or B and point to one specific detail. 選一張，指出一個細節。
  • Make v0 and save both the prompt and image. 留下第一版與實際提示。
  • Inspect one issue, revise one instruction, save v1 and check again. 改一件事，再比一次。
  • Make one change for your chosen reader and ask a peer to inspect it. 為讀者修改，請同學檢查。
  • Complete E1 with a decision, evidence, reason and next action. 完成判斷卡。

Make version 0 / 動手做第一版

Open Gemini. Find Images in the sidebar, or the image-generation entry available in your account. Paste this prompt, the written request you give AI. The exact interface may differ. Google image instructions, checked 10 September 2026.

開啟 Gemini 生圖入口，貼上提示（prompt）；以自己帳號顯示的介面為準。

Create a portrait poster for a FICTIONAL campus book swap for first-year students, including international students. Use a warm paper background, coral accents and an illustration of books. Make the event details easy to read on a phone.
Use these facts exactly: BOOK SWAP; 22 SEPT 2026; 16:30; LIBRARY LOBBY; BRING ONE BOOK, TAKE ONE BOOK.
Do not add a school logo, official endorsement, real person, sponsor or registration link. Mark it FICTIONAL EVENT / CLASS EXERCISE. If a detail is missing, ask instead of inventing it.

Save the actual prompt and download or screenshot the image as v0. If you added a condition, record it. An AI message saying “done” is not the image evidence.

保存實際提示與 v0 圖像；有加條件，也一併記下。

No payment is required. Google lists image generation without an AI subscription, but availability and limits can change. We do not promise a fixed number of images. Personal-account image generation and editing have different age conditions; editing requires age 18 or above. School accounts may have administrator restrictions. If access, age, quota or features block you, take the offline route below. Do not pay or bypass restrictions. Limits · Sign-in requirements, checked 10 September 2026.

不必付費。生圖與編輯有不同年齡限制；編輯需滿 18 歲。帳號或額度受阻，改走離線路線。

Use only the fictional brief. Do not upload student records, private photos or material you cannot use. Data use depends on your settings and account; turning off activity does not mean no data is processed. Gemini privacy information, checked 10 September 2026.

只用虛構內容；不貼個資、私人照片或未授權素材。

Offline route / 帳號或裝置受阻

Open the offline studio. Choose A and select Save v0 before editing. Change a field, then save v1 and your notes. Check your Downloads folder. These files are not submitted automatically.

先存原版，再修改、存修正版與紀錄；下載不等於繳交。

A, B and C are editable SVG teaching mockups made for this package, not Gemini test results or event photos. A says 18:30; B changes it to 16:30; C adds an unauthorized “OFFICIAL NDHU EVENT” claim. Make and explain your own decision. Identify the source as “teaching mockup + my edits.”

素材是教學模擬，不是 Gemini 實跑圖；來源要如實標記。

Without a laptop, share a screen or use printed A/B/C sheets. Circle a detail, write a revision instruction, sketch the revision and complete the same E1. Access barriers do not lower your mark and do not create a requirement to rerun AI later.

沒有筆電可共用畫面或紙本，完成相同判斷；不需課後補跑 AI。

Test and revise / 檢查，再修一件事

Compare your own image with the fixed facts. Inspect the time and place. View it at phone width to check readability. Look for invented endorsements or links. Circle or screenshot one actual issue.

對照固定資訊，縮到手機寬度，圈出一個實際問題。

If the time really is wrong, try:

Keep the layout and all other content. Change only the event time to 16:30. Do not add information.

If the time is already right, use your observed issue instead. If your first image passes the checks, record a passed test and choose an improvement for the reader. Do not invent a failure to fill the card.

沒有錯誤就記錄通過的檢查，不要捏造失敗。

Save v1. Check whether the requested change happened and whether anything else changed. If it remains wrong, record the remaining issue and choose another request or a manual edit. Adding a text box in Slides, PowerPoint or LibreOffice Impress is valid; keep v0 and disclose the manual change.

修完再比一次；可以手動加文字方塊，但要保留原版並註明。

Make it yours / 做成自己的版本

Return to the tool or offline-studio tab you were just using and continue with your own v1. Do not open a fresh studio for this step: it starts again with mockup A. In Gemini, use the conversation for your image; if needed, find the image in Library and choose Chat. Keep the title BOOK SWAP. After your change, save the revised image and update your notes.

回到剛才的工具或工作室分頁，接著自己的 v1 修改，不重開素材。保留 BOOK SWAP；改完再保存圖像與紀錄。

Choose a more specific reader, such as international freshmen or graphic-novel readers. Change one tagline, color choice or visual emphasis while keeping the event facts. Avoid stereotypes.

選一種讀者，改一個視覺決定，保留活動事實。

Ask a peer to say: “At ___, I see ___. For our reader, this means ___.” They should point to the image. Accept their suggestion or explain why you do not.

請同學指到圖上的位置；你可以採用，也可以說明不採用的理由。

E1: your judgment / 圖像判斷卡

Open the blank E1 card. Type and print/save as PDF, or print and write by hand. It does not save or submit automatically.

E1 可以打字後列印或手寫；不會自動保存、送出。

Record the inspected version; adopt, modify or reject; one visible detail and its location; why it matters for the brief or reader; your next action and check; and the source/tool/mockup plus your own changes. All three decisions are valid with evidence.

寫下版本、決定、細節、理由、下一步，以及素材與人的修改。

Complete and submit E1 in class. Follow the teacher’s class and e-learning announcement for submission. This material adds no new percentage, deadline or public-posting requirement.

當堂完成、當堂繳交；依老師公告，不另加配分或公開要求。
